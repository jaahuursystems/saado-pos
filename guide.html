Saado POS

Hagaha Shaqaalaha (hal bog)

1. Gal

  1. Fur app-ka Saado POS.
  2. Taabo magacaaga, geli PIN-kaaga, taabo Gal.
  3. Haddii aad PIN khaldan 5 jeer geliso, sug 5 daqiiqo.

2. Bilow wareegga

  1. Taabo Wareeg.
  2. Tiri lacagta khasnadda, eeg haraaga Zaad iyo Edahab, geli.
  3. Taabo Bilow wareegga. Hadda waad iibin kartaa.

3. Iibi

  1. Taabo alaabta (badhamada waaweyn), ama sawir baarkoodka 📷, ama qor magaca.
  2. Sawir badan: 📷 sii wad sawirka — cod kasta hal shay ayuu ku darayaa.
  3. Taabo Bixi. Dooro Caddaan / Zaad / Edahab / Deyn.
  4. Caddaan: geli lacagta uu bixiyey (SH ama USD) — baaqiga waa la tusayaa SH.
  5. Zaad / Edahab: macmiilka tus lambarka akoonka. Hubi in lacagtu timid.
  6. Taabo Dhammee iibka.
  7. Macmiil kale ayaa yimid? Taabo dambiisha → Dhig. Dabadeed ka soo celi badhanka ⏸.

4. Internet la'aan

Iibku wuu shaqaynayaa xitaa internet la'aan. Badhanka sare wuxuu muujinayaa ⏳ inta sugaysa. Marka internet-ku yimaado si toos ah ayaa loo dirayaa. Ha ka bixin ka hor inta aan la dirin.

5. Deyn iyo lacag-bixin

  • Iib deyn ah: dooro Deyn, dooro macmiilka (ama ＋ macmiil cusub).
  • Macmiil deyn bixinaya: Macaamiil → magaca → Qaado lacag.

6. Kharash iyo celin

  • Kharash (koronto, gaarsiin…): Wax kale → Kharashaad.
  • Alaab la soo celiyey: Wax kale → Celin lacag → Codso, qor sababta. Milkiilaha ayaa ansixinaya.

7. Fast Food — dhammaadka maalinta

Wax kale → Agabka cuntada → Isticmaalka maanta: geli inta bur, saliid, baradho iyo digaag la isticmaalay.

8. Xir wareegga

  1. Taabo Wareeg. Eeg "Caddaanka la filayo".
  2. Tiri lacagta, geli caddaanka, Zaad iyo Edahab.
  3. Taabo Xir wareegga. Waxaad arkaysaa haddii ay sax tahay, dheeraad ama dhiman.

Beddel PIN-kaaga: Wax kale → PIN-kayga. PIN-kaaga cidna ha u sheegin.

Employee guide (one page)

1. Log in

  1. Open the Saado POS app.
  2. Tap your name, type your PIN, tap Enter.
  3. 5 wrong PINs lock the account for 5 minutes.

2. Start your shift

  1. Tap Shift.
  2. Count the cash drawer, check the Zaad and Edahab balances, type them in.
  3. Tap Start shift. Now you can sell.

3. Sell

  1. Tap items (big buttons), or scan the barcode 📷, or type the name.
  2. Many items: keep the 📷 open — each beep adds one item.
  3. Tap Pay. Choose Cash / Zaad / Edahab / Credit.
  4. Cash: type what the customer gave (SH or USD) — change is shown in SH.
  5. Zaad / Edahab: show the customer the account number. Check the money arrived.
  6. Tap Complete sale.
  7. Another customer is waiting? Tap the cart → Park. Bring it back later with the ⏸ button.

4. No internet

Selling still works. The top button shows ⏳ and how many are waiting. They are sent automatically when the internet returns. Do not log out before they are sent.

5. Credit and payments

  • Credit sale: choose Credit, pick the customer (or ＋ new customer).
  • Customer pays debt: Customers → name → Receive payment.

6. Expenses and refunds

  • Expense (electricity, delivery…): More → Expenses.
  • Returned item: More → Refunds → Request, write the reason. An owner approves it.

7. Fast Food — end of day

More → Ingredients → End of day usage: type how much flour, oil, potatoes and chicken were used.

8. End your shift

  1. Tap Shift. Look at "Expected cash".
  2. Count the money, type cash, Zaad and Edahab.
  3. Tap End shift. You see if it is correct, extra or short.

Change your PIN: More → My PIN. Never tell anyone your PIN.

Hagaha Milkiilaha

Shaqada maalinlaha

  • Hoyga: iibka maanta (Dukaan, Fast Food, ama labadaba), faa'iidada, caddaan/Zaad/Edahab/deyn, digniinaha (kayd yar, dhacaya, agab yar, codsiyo celin).
  • Badhanka sare Dukaan / Cunto ayaa beddelaya ganacsiga aad ka shaqaynayso.
  • Soo koobidda maanta ku dir WhatsApp: geli lambarkaaga Dejinta → "Lambarka WhatsApp".

Alaabta

  • Wax kale → Alaabta → ＋ Cusub. Baarkoodka 📷 ku sawir. ⭐ = had iyo jeer muuji shaashadda iibka.
  • Alaab badan: Soo geli CSV / Excel. Marka hore Soo deji tusaalaha, ku buuxi Excel, kadib soo geli. Baarkood isku mid ah = waa la cusboonaysiinayaa.
  • Tirinta kaydka: sawir shay kasta, qor tirada dhabta ah, kaydi.

Alaab-keenayaasha

  • Iibsi cusub: dooro alaabta, tirada iyo qiimaha. Kaydku wuu kordhayaa, qiimaha iibsiguna wuu cusboonaanayaa. Inta aan la bixin waxay noqonaysaa deyn aan ku leenahay.
  • Ku dalbo WhatsApp: "Ku dar dhammaan alaabta yaraatay" → PDF ayaa loo dirayaa alaab-keenaha.
  • Lacagta alaab-keenaha loo bixiyo si toos ah ayay u galaysaa Kharashaadka.

Isticmaalayaasha (Wax kale → Isticmaalayaasha)

  • Ku dar shaqaale: magac, meesha uu ka shaqeeyo, luqadda, oggolaanshaha. PIN ayaa la sameynayaa — hal mar ayaa la muujinayaa.
  • Samee PIN cusub haddii qof illoobo. Ka saar calaamadda "Akoonku waa shaqaynayaa" si aad u xirto.
  • "Saacadaha ka baxsan": shaqaale caadi ah ma geli karo saacadaha shaqada ka baxsan (Dukaan 8–1 iyo 4–11, Fast Food 5–12) haddii aanad oggolaan.

Dejinta

Sicirka USD, soo dhoweynta (500), akoonnada Zaad/Edahab, deyn ON/OFF, noocyada dalabka Fast Food, saacadaha, waqtiga xiritaanka Fast Food, noocyada kharashka.

Warbixinnada

Iibka maalinlaha (sida loo bixiyey), faa'iidada, alaabta ugu iibka badan, iibka shaqaale kasta, kharashaadka, deynta macaamiisha, kayd yar, haraaga lacagta wareegyada. Faa'iido = iib saafi ah − qiimaha iibsiga alaabta − kharashaadka. Geli qiimaha iibsiga (cost) alaab kasta si faa'iidadu u saxnaato.

Kaydinta (backup)

Habeen kasta 3:00 nuqul ayaa la galiyaa Google Drive galka Saado POS Backups (30 maalmood + bil kasta 24 bilood). Dejinta → Hadda kaydi nuqul.

Ereyada Soomaaliga ah: fur faylka i18n.js ee GitHub, beddel qoraalka midig, kaydi. Kadibna kor u qaad lambarka nooca ee sw.js.

Owner guide

Daily use

  • Home: today's sales (Shop, Fast Food or both), profit, cash/Zaad/Edahab/credit, and alerts (low stock, expiring, low ingredients, refund requests).
  • The Shop / Food switch at the top changes which business you work in.
  • Send daily summary on WhatsApp: put your number in Settings → "WhatsApp number".

Products

  • More → Products → ＋ New. Scan the barcode with 📷. ⭐ = always show on the sell screen.
  • Many products: Import CSV / Excel. First Download template, fill it in Excel, then import. Same barcode = updated, not duplicated.
  • Stock count: scan each item, type the real count, save. Differences are logged.

Suppliers

  • New purchase: pick items, quantity and unit cost. Stock goes up and cost price updates. The unpaid part becomes what we owe.
  • Order on WhatsApp: "Add all low-stock items" → a PDF order is sent to the supplier.
  • Money paid to suppliers goes into Expenses automatically.

Users (More → Users)

  • Add staff: name, where they work, language, permissions. A PIN is created and shown once.
  • Make a new PIN if someone forgets. Untick "Account active" to disable.
  • "Outside shift hours": employees cannot use the app outside working hours (Shop 8–1 and 4–11, Fast Food 5–12) unless you allow it.

Settings

USD rate, rounding (500), Zaad/Edahab accounts, credit ON/OFF, Fast Food order types, hours, Fast Food closing time, expense categories.

Reports

Daily sales by payment, profit, best sellers, sales per employee, expenses, customer debts, low stock, money balances from shifts. Profit = net sales − cost of goods sold − expenses. Enter a cost price for each product so profit is right.

Backups

Every night at 3:00 a copy goes to the Google Drive folder Saado POS Backups (30 days + one per month for 24 months). Settings → Back up now.

Somali words: open i18n.js on GitHub, change the text on the right, save, then raise the version number in sw.js.
